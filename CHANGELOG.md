# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-31)

First release: eight skills for 3D and animated websites.

- `threejs-scene`: code-built three.js scenes with a 60 fps starter (sky and sun presets, fitted shadows, GTAO, bloom, instancing kit, profile sweeps, quality tiers, recording hooks).
- `scroll-film`: scroll-scrubbed films from frame sequences or scrub-ready video, with holds, smoothing and reduced motion; prep and seam tools.
- `motion-assets`: planning and generating AI assets; loop checker and outpaint paste-back.
- `alpha-cutout`: per-frame chroma keying with edge unmixing; stacked-alpha MP4 player for Safari.
- `cursor-rig`: cursor-following head rigs from head-turn clips; builder and player.
- `exploded-view`: staged explode, x-ray, section, labels and picking; motor demo.
- `site-check`: Chromium and WebKit checks at three sizes with a contact sheet.
- `frame-capture`: virtual-clock recorder, comparison builder, Range-capable server.
- Install for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and OpenCode with one command.
